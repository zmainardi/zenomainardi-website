ABOUT

Music Producer and Audio Engineer in Berlin

With a degree from SAE in New York, I run a high-end studio in the heart of Berlin. With over 20 years of experience, I’ve worked across film, advertising, and music, collaborating with both companies and established as well as independent artists.

Clear communication and a structured workflow ensure your project is handled with precision—from the first idea to the final release. You’re welcome to visit the studio in Berlin-Mitte for an in-person session or collaborate remotely, depending on what best supports your process.

My services include production, mixing, mastering, and recording across a wide range of genres. With extensive experience in film soundtracks and advertising music, I also offer post-production for movies, documentaries, and commercials.

As a synthesizer enthusiast, the studio features a curated selection of analog synths and rare electronic instruments to give your project a distinctive character.

BUDGET AND FILE SENDING GUIDELINES

Files can be sent via WeTransfer, Dropbox or FTP — ZIP via WeTransfer preferred

A

BUDGET & REVISIONS

Contact me with your project needs and I will provide an estimate. Typically, 50% is due upon agreement to secure the project. This becomes non-refundable once work has commenced, and the remaining balance is due on completion.

Three revisions are included. Additional revisions, file substitutions after work has started, alternate loudness versions, additional formats and extra versions may incur additional charges.

B

WHEN SENDING FILES FOR MIXING

  1. Make a reference bounce of your mix with master-bus plugins on, if any, in AIFF/WAV.
  2. Turn off master-bus plugins before bouncing individual tracks or stems.
  3. Send files at the same sample rate as your project.
  4. Use the highest possible bit depth supported by your DAW: 32-bit or 24-bit.
  5. Send mono tracks as mono files and stereo tracks as stereo files.
  6. Check whether “analog” plugin modes are creating unnecessary noise.
  7. Do not normalize the files.
  8. Bounce consolidated AIFF/WAV files with the same length as the project.
  9. Bounce effect aux channels to separate files.
  10. Name individual files clearly.
  11. Send song tempo and key information if possible.
  12. OMF or an open project can also be used when discussed first.
C

WHEN SENDING FILES FOR MASTERING

  • Most of the mixing-delivery notes also apply to mastering.
  • Aim for approximately -10 dB peak level; a little hotter is fine.
  • For stem mastering, separate bass, vocals, kick and the rest of the drums where appropriate.
  • Send high-resolution reference tracks that represent the sound and loudness you want.
